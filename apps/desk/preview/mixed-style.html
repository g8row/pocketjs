PocketDeck: mixed style

One rule on every page: read-only → terminal square frames with the title in the border, mono type, ASCII bars and sparklines; touchable → rounded pills, tiles, chips, sliders, toggles. Icons are single-colour Nerd Font glyphs (loaded from the real symbols font), tinted like text. Screens are the tablet's 512×300 layout at 1.5×; the left strip is the rail over the damaged area.
